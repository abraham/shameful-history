On January 3, 2026, the United States launched large-scale military strikes in Venezuela, targeting Caracas and other sites to remove President Nicolás Maduro and his wife, Cilia Flores, from power. The operation marked a significant escalation, with the U.S. citing charges of narcoterrorism against Maduro. Venezuelan officials condemned the attack as an imperialist aggression.
